Commands

After inviting the bot, type /help in your server. The buttons inside that menu are connected to the bot.

/helpOpen the interactive Omni Cave menu.
/ai question:Ask Omni Cave AI.
/warn member: reason:Warn a member.
/warnings member:View warning history.
/mute member: duration:Timeout a member.
/ban member:Ban a member.
/unban user_id:Unban by Discord user ID.
/role member: role:Add or remove a role.
/levelView XP and level.
/leaderboardView the top 10.
/setqotd channel:Choose the QOTD channel.
/qotdPost today's QOTD.
